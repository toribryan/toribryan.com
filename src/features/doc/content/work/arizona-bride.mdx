## The audit

I audited the site's core templates, including the homepage, article pages,
vendor listings, and gallery experiences, and found three problems working
against the brand:

- **Navigation complexity.** Content categories overlapped and lacked
  hierarchy, making it hard for a reader to move between inspiration, vendor
  discovery, and editorial content.
- **Visual inconsistency.** Typography, spacing, and imagery were dated and
  lacked cohesion, weakening brand authority in a highly aesthetic industry.
- **Ad intrusiveness.** Oversized ad placements disrupted reading flow and
  reduced content immersion.

## A clearer information architecture

I restructured the taxonomy around clearer top-level categories: Wedding Pros
& Venues, Fashion & Beauty, Weddings, Events, and Shop, with Podcasts, Sign Up,
and Log In surfaced separately from the content categories. The goals were to
simplify primary navigation, establish intuitive pathways between editorial
and vendor content, and reduce the cognitive load on high-traffic pages, so a
reader can move seamlessly between inspiration and action.

## Impact

While the project is still in development, the new structure is already built
for growth:

- Increased ad placement opportunities
- Improved content discoverability
- Stronger brand positioning in a competitive bridal market
