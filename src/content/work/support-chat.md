## The problem

A proctored exam has stakes and a clock. When a test-taker hits friction, the standard path is leave the exam, email support, wait. By the time help arrives, the exam is over.

## The solution

A support chat that lives inside the exam UI, understands where the test-taker is, and can route to a human without disrupting the session. Chat state persists if the session is paused.

## Impact

The majority of "I couldn't finish because ___" tickets no longer exist. They're resolved in-session.
